Kwang-tae Eum

Author of Tarot Digest and Business I Ching · Founder of Zeus Oracle

Kwang-tae Eum (음광태) is a Korean author who has spent years studying the tarot and the I Ching side by side. His work treats both not as fortune-telling tools but as symbolic languages for understanding people — their choices, fears and turning points.

His books read each tarot card through myth, history and human psychology, and pair it with the matching wisdom of the I Ching. The aim is practical: to help readers ask a better question about their own lives.

The Zeus Oracle app grew out of that approach. Every reflection on this site is built on the card meanings developed in his books.
