The Devil Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
XV
Hebrew letter
Ayin (ע)
Astrology
Capricorn
Keywords
Attachment, temptation, bondage, materialism
Short answerThe Devil shows the chains we accept. Addiction, unhealthy attachments and fear can feel binding — but the card reveals that the chains are looser than they look.

A horned figure crouches on a pedestal. Below him a man and woman stand chained by the neck — yet the chains hang loosely enough to lift off.

That detail is the whole message: much of what binds us is something we have agreed to.

Upright meaning

  • Unhealthy attachment or dependency
  • Temptation and indulgence
  • Feeling trapped by money, habit or fear
  • Facing your shadow side

Reversed meaning

  • Breaking free from a bad pattern
  • Reclaiming your power
  • Recognizing what controls you
  • Detachment and release

In love, money, career & health

Love

Intense attraction or a relationship based on dependence. Ask whether it frees you or binds you.

Money

Debt, overspending or chasing material security. Look at what drives it.

Career

A job you stay in only out of fear. Know your options.

Health

Watch habits and dependencies. Small changes loosen big chains.

The single question this card asks

“Are you ready to admit that the chains are loose?”

Frequently asked questions

What does the Devil tarot card mean?

The Devil represents attachment, temptation and self-imposed bondage — patterns that feel binding but can be broken.

Is the Devil a yes or no card?

Usually no, or a warning to examine your motives first.

What does the Devil mean in love?

It can point to strong physical attraction, codependency or a relationship that feels hard to leave.

What does the Devil reversed mean?

Reversed, it often signals release — breaking free from an unhealthy pattern.
