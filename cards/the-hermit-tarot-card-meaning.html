The Hermit Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
IX
Hebrew letter
Yod (י)
Astrology
Virgo
Keywords
Solitude, introspection, wisdom, guidance
Short answerThe Hermit withdraws to find the truth. He lights only the next step of the road — and asks you to trust the wisdom you have earned through your own experience.

An old man in a grey cloak stands alone on a snowy peak, holding a lantern with a six-pointed star and leaning on a staff.

He has climbed above the noise. His light is not for show; it is enough to see the next step, and enough to guide others who follow.

Upright meaning

  • Introspection and time alone
  • Seeking deeper meaning
  • Inner guidance and wisdom
  • Mentorship — receiving or giving

Reversed meaning

  • Isolation or loneliness
  • Withdrawing to avoid problems
  • Refusing advice
  • Losing your sense of direction

In love, money, career & health

Love

Time to reflect on what you truly want. Space is not the same as rejection.

Money

Review finances carefully and avoid crowd-driven decisions.

Career

Specialized, independent or research work. Seek a mentor or become one.

Health

Rest and quiet restore you. Watch for isolation becoming loneliness.

The single question this card asks

“Can you trust the wisdom you have gained through your own experience?”

In Tarot Digest, the Hermit is read alongside the I Ching hexagram Modesty (Qian): a mountain that places itself beneath the earth — holding something great without displaying it.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Hermit tarot card mean?

The Hermit represents introspection, solitude and inner wisdom — stepping back to find your own truth.

Is the Hermit a yes or no card?

Usually 'not yet'. Take time to reflect before acting.

What does the Hermit mean in love?

It can mean a period of being single by choice, or a partner who needs space to think.

What does the Hermit reversed mean?

Reversed, it warns of isolation, loneliness, or avoiding the reflection you need.
