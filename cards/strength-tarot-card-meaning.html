Strength Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
VIII
Hebrew letter
Teth (ט)
Astrology
Leo
Keywords
Inner strength, courage, patience, gentle mastery
Short answerStrength is courage without force. A gentle hand calms the lion — the card asks you to master fear and anger with patience rather than suppression.

A woman in white, crowned with flowers and an infinity sign above her head, calmly closes the jaws of a lion. There is no struggle in her posture.

This is power that does not need to raise its voice: compassion, self-control and steady confidence.

Upright meaning

  • Inner courage and resilience
  • Patience and compassion
  • Taming impulses and strong emotions
  • Quiet confidence

Reversed meaning

  • Self-doubt or low confidence
  • Suppressed emotions that may erupt
  • Losing your temper
  • Feeling overwhelmed by fear

In love, money, career & health

Love

Patience and kindness strengthen the bond. Soft words calm conflict.

Money

Hold steady and avoid impulsive moves driven by fear or greed.

Career

Lead with calm confidence. Persistence wins over aggression.

Health

Good resilience and recovery. Address stress gently but consistently.

The single question this card asks

“Are you ready to govern without fighting?”

Frequently asked questions

What does the Strength tarot card mean?

Strength represents inner courage, patience and the gentle mastery of fear and impulse.

Is Strength a yes or no card?

Yes, especially when patience and self-control are required.

What does Strength mean in love?

It points to a relationship strengthened by compassion, patience and mutual respect.

What does Strength reversed mean?

Reversed, it suggests self-doubt or emotions held in so long they are close to erupting.
