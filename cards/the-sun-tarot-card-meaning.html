The Sun Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
XIX
Hebrew letter
Resh (ר)
Astrology
Sun
Keywords
Joy, success, vitality, clarity, warmth
Short answerThe Sun is pure joy and success. Things are clear, warm and going well — the card asks whether you are ready to accept happiness when it arrives.

A smiling child rides a white horse beneath a radiant sun, holding a red banner. Sunflowers rise above a garden wall behind him.

After the uncertainty of the Moon comes full daylight: nothing hidden, everything alive.

Upright meaning

  • Happiness and success
  • Vitality and good health
  • Clarity and confidence
  • Celebration and warmth

Reversed meaning

  • Temporary clouds over good fortune
  • Overconfidence
  • Difficulty enjoying success
  • Delayed happiness

In love, money, career & health

Love

A happy, warm relationship. Joy shared openly.

Money

Financial success and stability.

Career

Recognition, achievement and visibility.

Health

Strong vitality and recovery.

The single question this card asks

“Are you ready to accept happiness when it arrives?”

Frequently asked questions

What does the Sun tarot card mean?

The Sun represents joy, success and vitality — one of the most positive cards in tarot.

Is the Sun a yes or no card?

A clear yes.

What does the Sun mean in love?

It suggests a happy, healthy relationship, and sometimes engagement or family joy.

What does the Sun reversed mean?

Reversed, it still leans positive, but joy may be delayed or dimmed by doubt.
