The Chariot Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
VII
Hebrew letter
Cheth (ח)
Astrology
Cancer
Keywords
Willpower, determination, direction, victory
Short answerThe Chariot is victory through focus. Two forces pull in different directions; the card asks you to gather your will and steer them toward one clear goal.

An armored figure stands in a chariot beneath a starry canopy, drawn by two sphinxes — one black, one white. He holds no reins; he guides them by will alone.

The card is about control of opposing impulses. Success comes not from more strength, but from a single direction.

Upright meaning

  • Determination and focused effort
  • Overcoming obstacles through willpower
  • Travel, movement and progress
  • Victory after struggle

Reversed meaning

  • Lack of direction; pulled in two ways
  • Aggression or forcing outcomes
  • Losing control of a situation
  • Stalled plans

In love, money, career & health

Love

Work through differences with commitment. Don't try to win the relationship like a contest.

Money

Disciplined pursuit of a clear target brings results.

Career

Ambition pays off. Set a goal and drive toward it.

Health

Determination helps recovery. Balance push with rest.

The single question this card asks

“Are you ready to gather your wavering heart and direct it toward a single goal?”

Frequently asked questions

What does the Chariot tarot card mean?

The Chariot represents willpower, determination and victory through focus and self-control.

Is the Chariot a yes or no card?

Yes — success comes if you stay focused and committed.

What does the Chariot mean in love?

It suggests moving a relationship forward with commitment, or overcoming obstacles together.

What does the Chariot reversed mean?

Reversed, it points to scattered energy, lost control, or forcing things too hard.
