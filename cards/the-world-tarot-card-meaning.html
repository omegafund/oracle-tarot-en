The World Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
XXI
Hebrew letter
Tav (ת)
Astrology
Saturn
Keywords
Completion, wholeness, achievement, integration
Short answerThe World is completion. A cycle has closed successfully — and the card asks whether you are ready to begin the next one.

A dancing figure floats within a wreath, holding a wand in each hand. In the four corners are a man, an eagle, a lion and a bull.

Everything has come together. Yet the wreath is a circle: every completion is also the start of a new journey.

Upright meaning

  • Completion and fulfillment
  • Achievement and recognition
  • Wholeness and integration
  • Travel or global connection

Reversed meaning

  • Unfinished business
  • Lack of closure
  • Fear of the next step
  • Delays near the finish

In love, money, career & health

Love

A fulfilling relationship or a major milestone together.

Money

Goals reached. Consolidate before the next cycle.

Career

Successful completion of a project or stage; international opportunities.

Health

Wholeness and balance; recovery complete.

The single question this card asks

“Now that this circle is complete, am I ready to begin the next one?”

In Tarot Digest, the World is read with the last two hexagrams of the I Ching — After Completion (Ji Ji) and Before Completion (Wei Ji): the world does not end with completion.

Frequently asked questions

What does the World tarot card mean?

The World represents completion, achievement and wholeness — the successful end of a cycle.

Is the World a yes or no card?

Yes. It is one of the most favorable outcomes.

What does the World mean in love?

It suggests fulfillment, commitment or a major milestone.

What does the World reversed mean?

Reversed, it points to incomplete goals or a missing sense of closure.
