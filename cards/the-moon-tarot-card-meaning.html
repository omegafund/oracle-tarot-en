The Moon Tarot Card Meaning

From the author of Tarot Digest · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Arcana
Major Arcana
Number
XVIII
Hebrew letter
Qoph (ק)
Astrology
Pisces
Keywords
Illusion, uncertainty, intuition, fear, the subconscious
Short answerThe Moon is the path through uncertainty. Not everything is clear, fears may distort what you see — the card asks you to move forward anyway, guided by intuition.

Under a full moon, a dog and a wolf howl on either side of a winding path. A crayfish crawls out of a pool, and two towers stand in the distance.

In moonlight, shapes shift. The card speaks of dreams, anxieties and half-truths — and of the courage to walk on without full sight.

Upright meaning

  • Uncertainty and ambiguity
  • Fear and anxiety
  • Intuition and dreams
  • Hidden or unclear information

Reversed meaning

  • Confusion beginning to clear
  • Facing fears
  • Deception revealed
  • Releasing anxiety

In love, money, career & health

Love

Mixed signals or unspoken fears. Don't fill gaps with worst-case stories.

Money

Avoid decisions based on rumors. Verify everything.

Career

Unclear situation at work. Trust your instincts but gather facts.

Health

Stress and sleep issues. Pay attention to emotional wellbeing.

The single question this card asks

“Are you ready to move forward even without clarity?”

Frequently asked questions

What does the Moon tarot card mean?

The Moon represents uncertainty, illusion and the subconscious — moving through confusion guided by intuition.

Is the Moon a yes or no card?

Usually 'unclear'. Wait for more information if you can.

What does the Moon mean in love?

It can point to confusion, insecurity or hidden feelings.

What does the Moon reversed mean?

Reversed, it suggests confusion lifting and fears losing their grip.
